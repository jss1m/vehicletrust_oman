# Expected Impact

Socially, the concept could improve confidence in vehicle identity evidence and make selected plate swapping, cloning, and reuse easier to detect. Investigation/recovery workflows could gain an explicit distinction between authentic identity, current binding, and reported-stolen state. Fleet and rental operators could gain auditable plate/vehicle lifecycle controls. The prototype also demonstrates locally implementable cryptographic identity engineering. These are expected future effects, not measured outcomes; no monetary or reduction percentage is claimed without a pilot.
