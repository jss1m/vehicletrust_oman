Prototype designed to detect selected vehicle identity attacks including credential cloning, plate transfer, tampering and replay.

What VehicleTrust Protects Against

Plate swapping, cloned credentials, genuine plates on the wrong vehicle, credential tampering, replay, revoked or lost physical plates, and reported-stolen vehicle status.

What It Does Not Directly Prevent

Relay attacks against vehicle keys, CAN-injection theft, physical towing, GPS jamming, theft with genuine vehicle credentials, or physical compromise of vehicle hardware.
